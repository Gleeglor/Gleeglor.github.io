Protocol Kardashev

2024-2026 Dev build

A diggable colony sandbox about waking up after a crash, scraping a living planet for scrap, and climbing the Kardashev scale one hard-won watt at a time. This page covers an early development build: playable, unfinished, and still changing.

Crash first, ambition later

The fantasy is simple and a little mean. You are not a god with a blank planet. You are a probe that already failed once. Vision is short. Systems are cracked. Scrap sits in the dirt like an argument you have to answer with drones, research, and patience.

The long promise is the Kardashev ladder: dirt digs become factories, factories become ships, ships become rings and stations that treat planets as parts. The short promise is more intimate. Can you make the next meter of crust useful before the fog eats your confidence?

A circle of sight in the mist

Colony play starts under soft circular fog. What you have not earned stays unread. Outside the vision dome, the world does not hand you free intel on ores and structures.

Tile probe is the close read: stone, ore, debris, empty air. Processes is the wide read: the scrap-to-stock graph that shows whether invent hubs and scrap processors are actually moving material. Both sit as panels you can drag while the planet keeps turning underneath.

Mycelium before the map opens

Early tools stay dark on purpose. Until mycelium makes sense, the growth network stays out of reach and construction without the right drones stays blocked. Research is not flavor text. It is the permission slip for the next layer of the planet.

Past that gate, a theoretical physics route sketches ore mining, smelting, and fabrication. Some of that climb already shapes the build catalog. Some of it is still arriving. Either way, the colony is meant to feel gated by understanding, not by a random unlock drip.

Pipelines you can watch

If a building is working, the overview should show the work. Buffers, bot use, power draw, and yields belong on the panel you already opened, so the sim never feels like a black box humming behind a static card.

Building ships

Construction turns landers and vessels into assemblies on a grid. Blocks connect, rotate, and carry systems. Hull plates are denser than frames. Thrusters add real push. Ghost placement tints green when a piece can attach and red when it cannot.

You can save full hull designs, or smaller blueprints to reuse later. When a new colony starts, the built-in Colony lander sits at the top of the picker, with custom landers waiting underneath if you have made any.

Looking farther out, the same ideas stretch toward ring slices, orbital rings, interplanetary stations, and star rings. Each step up that ladder is enormous. The long fantasy stays huge even while the early colony stays small enough to hold in your hands.

Reading a single pixel

Inside vision, the probe is where the next dig or build decision happens. Outside vision, it stays quiet on purpose. The map only tells you what your colony has earned the right to know.

Still a workshop

This is an early development build. Systems arrive in playable slices while the long climb (from crash site to megastructures) stays the north star. Some routes are already in your hands. Others are still taking shape. Fog, scrap, research, ship building, and a ladder that keeps pointing at the stars.
